CAMPUSBITE

Terms of Service

CampusBite lets GIIT students browse the canteen menu, place orders and follow order updates. Menu availability and prices are provided by the canteen and may change. Orders are subject to canteen acceptance and availability.

Online payments are handled by Razorpay. CampusBite does not collect or store card details. For order support, contact the canteen staff.
CAMPUSBITE

Privacy Policy

CampusBite stores the name, email address and phone number you provide to manage your account, orders and order updates. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. Order and payment status are used to fulfil and track orders.

Payments are processed by Razorpay; CampusBite does not store card details. Staff can access order information needed to prepare and manage canteen orders.
